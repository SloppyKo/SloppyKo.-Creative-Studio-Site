# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how Kieran Apgar, doing business as SloppyKo ("SloppyKo," "we," "us," or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies on https://www.sloppyko.com (the "Site"). It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](https://www.sloppyko.com/privacy).

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, web beacons, pixels, and tags. Websites use them to remember preferences, keep users signed in, measure traffic, or track activity for advertising.

---

## 2. Our Short Answer

**The Site does not place cookies or similar tracking technologies on visitors' devices.**

We do not use:

- Advertising, targeting, or retargeting cookies
- Social media cookies or embedded social media plugins
- Tracking pixels, web beacons, or tags
- Local storage or session storage
- Third-party analytics cookies, such as Google Analytics

---

## 3. Analytics Without Cookies

We use Vercel Analytics to understand how the Site is used, such as which pages are visited and where visitors come from. Vercel Analytics is cookieless: it does not store anything on your device and does not track you across other websites. The information it collects is described in Section 1 of our Privacy Policy.

---

## 4. Administrative Area

The Site includes a private administrative area used only by the Site owner. That area uses strictly necessary authentication cookies to keep the owner securely signed in. These cookies are not set for Site visitors, and visitors cannot access that area.

---

## 5. Third-Party Websites

The Site contains plain links to third-party websites such as LinkedIn and YouTube. These links do not place cookies while you are on our Site. If you click through, those websites may set their own cookies under their own policies, which we do not control.

---

## 6. Managing Cookies

Because the Site does not set cookies for visitors, there is nothing to accept or decline, and we do not display a cookie consent banner. If you want to manage cookies on other websites, most browsers let you view, block, and delete cookies through their settings.

---

## 7. Updates to This Policy

If we add cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this policy before they are used, revise the "Last updated" date, and request your consent where the law requires it.
